About Wopzan

Wopzan, in Bijbehara, Anantnag, is a picturesque village known for green pastures, agriculture, and horticulture. The community thrives on hospitality, cultural richness, and shared growth. With modern amenities and deep-rooted traditions, Wopzan symbolizes rural Kashmir's harmony.
