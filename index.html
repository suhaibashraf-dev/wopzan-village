About Wopzan

Wopzan is a beautiful village located in Bijbehara of Anantnag, in the union territory of Jammu and Kashmir. Surrounded by the natural beauty of the Kashmir Valley, the village is known for its peaceful atmosphere, green fields, and hardworking people. Agriculture and horticulture play an important role in the lives of the residents, with many families engaged in farming and fruit cultivation. The people of Wopzan are known for their hospitality, strong community spirit, and rich cultural traditions. The village reflects the beauty and heritage of rural Kashmir, where traditional values, scenic landscapes, and a close-knit community create a pleasant and harmonious way of life.
